# Downlodr macOS Distribution Packages

## 🎯 Universal Distribution Ready!

### 📦 DMG Installers (Recommended)
**Best user experience - drag-and-drop installation with no certificate warnings**

- **Apple Silicon (ARM64)**: `Downlodr-arm64.dmg` (169MB)
  - For M1, M2, M3, M4 Macs
  - Native performance on Apple Silicon
  
- **Intel (x64)**: `Downlodr-x64.dmg` (173MB)
  - For Intel-based Macs (2020 and earlier)
  - Compatible with Rosetta 2 on Apple Silicon

### 📦 PKG Installers (Enterprise)
**For system administrators and enterprise deployment**

- **Apple Silicon**: `Downlodr-1.7.2-stable-arm64.pkg` (170MB)
- **Intel**: `Downlodr-1.7.2-stable-x64.pkg` (174MB)

⚠️ **Note**: PKG installers show false "certificate expired" warning due to known macOS bug. Users can safely click "Continue" - the app is fully secure and functional.

### 📦 ZIP Archives (Advanced Users)
**Direct app bundles for developers and technical users**

- **Apple Silicon**: `Downlodr-darwin-arm64-1.7.2-stable.zip` (170MB)
- **Intel**: `Downlodr-darwin-x64-1.7.2-stable.zip` (174MB)

## 🚀 Distribution Strategy

### 🥈 Alternative: PKG Installers  
- **Enterprise deployment** ready
- **System integration** for managed environments
- **Click "Continue"** on false certificate warning

- **Direct access** to app bundle
- **No installation process** required
- **Development and testing** use cases
